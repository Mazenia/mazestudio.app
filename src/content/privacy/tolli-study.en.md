title: Tolli Study privacy policy
description: Tolli Study does not collect or share personal data. Your cards stay on your phone.

Tolli Study is an Android app by Maze Studio. This policy explains what data Tolli Study uses and what it does with it. The short version: **Tolli Study has no server and no accounts, and Maze Studio never receives your cards or anything you study.**

## What's stored on your phone

Your subjects, topics and cards, the images you add to a card, your review history, the study session in progress and your settings. All of it stays on your phone. If you delete the app, all of that is deleted with it.

## Copies

- **Android backup:** if it's turned on for your phone, Android keeps a copy of the app's data in your Google account, as it does for other apps. Google handles it, not Maze Studio.
- **Export:** when you export a copy, Tolli Study saves it wherever you choose. It only leaves your phone if you move it.
- **Sync with your Drive (Pro, optional):** if you turn it on, Tolli Study keeps a copy in a hidden app folder inside your own Google Drive. It only asks for that permission (drive.appdata): it can't see or touch your other files. You can turn it off any time in Settings. The copy goes from your phone to your Drive; Maze Studio doesn't receive it.

## Adding cards with your AI

Tolli Study opens the AI app you choose (or copies the instructions) so you can ask it for cards. What you send to that AI, such as your notes or a photo, is covered by that service's privacy policy, not this one. Tolli Study only reads the clipboard when you tap "Paste".

## AnkiDroid

If you use your AnkiDroid decks and give permission, Tolli Study reads AnkiDroid's cards on your phone to ask them during breaks, and saves your answers in AnkiDroid. None of that leaves your phone through Tolli Study.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for the break questions.
- **Exact alarms:** so the bell rings when each block ends.
- **Run at startup:** to reschedule the session after the phone restarts.
- **Vibration:** for the bell on vibrate.
- **Internet:** to sync with your Drive, if you turn it on, and for Google Play purchases.
- **AnkiDroid:** only if you choose to use your AnkiDroid decks.

You pick the images for your cards one at a time; Tolli Study doesn't ask for access to all your photos. It doesn't ask for location, contacts or the microphone either.

## Feedback

"Tell us something" opens your email app. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it to reply and improve the app.

## Purchases

Tolli Study Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Maze Studio doesn't receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists.

## Children

Tolli Study is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect data from anyone, including them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes too. If Tolli Study ever started collecting any data, we would say so here before releasing that version.
