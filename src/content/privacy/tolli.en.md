Tolli is an Android app by Maze Studio. This policy explains what data Tolli uses and what it does with it. The short version: **Tolli does not collect, send or share personal data.**

## What stays on your phone

Your schedule, interval, days, sound, volume, theme and a log of today's dings. All of it stays on your phone. Tolli has no servers or accounts, and it rings without an internet connection. If you delete the app, all of it is deleted with it.

## Permissions

- **Exact alarms:** to ring at the exact time.
- **Run at startup:** to schedule the next ding again after a restart.
- **Vibration:** for the ding on vibrate.

Tolli also includes the internet permission because Google Play's payment system requires it. It is only used to talk to Google Play about Tolli Pro; Tolli sends nothing to Maze Studio or anyone else.

Tolli does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos.

## Saying the time

If you turn on "Say the time", Tolli uses the speech engine already on your phone. The spoken text ("It's 10:45") is not sent from the app to Maze Studio.

## Purchases

Tolli Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Maze Studio does not receive your payment details; Tolli only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists.

## Children

Tolli is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes too. If Tolli ever started collecting any data, we would say so here before releasing that version.
